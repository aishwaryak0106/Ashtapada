About Us

Empowering Young Minds Through Chess

ASHTAPADA CHESS ACADEMY is a charitable trust based in Kozhikode, Kerala, dedicated to developing young minds through chess education.

Founded by Atmaj C.H., the Academy offers online and offline chess coaching for students of all skill levels, helping improve focus, creativity, confidence, and strategic thinking.

We also conduct tournaments, workshops, and scholarship programs to support talented players and make chess accessible to everyone.
