About Us

Empowering Young Minds Through Chess

ASHTAPADA CHESS ACADEMY is a charitable initiative dedicated to nurturing young minds through chess, strategic thinking, and academic excellence.

Founded in Kerala, our academy provides both online and offline chess coaching programs designed to help students develop concentration, discipline, confidence, and decision-making skills.

Message from Our Founder

Chess is more than a game. It is a powerful educational tool that teaches patience, discipline, critical thinking, and problem-solving.

ASHTAPADA CHESS ACADEMY was established with a vision to provide every child an opportunity to learn these essential life skills through chess.

We believe that every move on the chessboard prepares a student for better decisions in life.

- Atmaj C.H

Our Mission

To develop strategic thinkers through chess education while promoting confidence, creativity, discipline, and lifelong learning.

Our Vision

To make quality chess education accessible to students across India and create future leaders through the power of chess.
