Our Programs

Chess Training Programs

Comprehensive chess coaching designed for beginners, intermediate players, and aspiring champions.

Beginner Program

Learn the fundamentals of chess, including piece movement, rules, tactics, and simple strategies. Perfect for children and newcomers to the game.

Intermediate Program

Strengthen tactical skills, opening principles, middlegame planning, and endgame techniques to improve tournament performance and rating.

Advanced & Professional Training

Intensive coaching for competitive players focusing on opening preparation, positional understanding, calculation, and psychological readiness.

Additional Activities

Beyond coaching, we provide opportunities to learn, compete, and grow.

Chess Tournaments

Regular tournaments to help students gain practical experience, build confidence, and showcase their skills.

Workshops & Seminars

Interactive sessions led by experienced coaches and experts covering advanced concepts and learning techniques.

Scholarships & Awards

Merit-based scholarships, certificates, and awards to recognize and support talented and deserving students.

About ASHTAPADA CHESS ACADEMY

A charitable initiative dedicated to promoting chess education, strategic thinking, and academic excellence throughout India.
