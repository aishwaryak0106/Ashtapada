Professional Chess Training & Certification Programs

At ASHTAPADA CHESS ACADEMY, we provide structured online and offline chess training programs designed for students at every skill level. Our coaching methodology focuses on strategic thinking, concentration, discipline, and competitive excellence.


Students receive comprehensive guidance in opening preparation, tactical combinations, positional understanding, endgame techniques, and tournament psychology. Our experienced coaches help each student build a strong foundation and progress toward achieving their personal and competitive goals.


Upon successful completion of our training modules, students are awarded certificates recognizing their dedication and progress. We also organize regular tournaments, workshops, and seminars to provide valuable practical experience and exposure.


Beyond chess mastery, our programs are designed to enhance memory, problem-solving ability, and academic performance. We believe chess is a powerful educational tool that prepares students for success both on and off the board.
