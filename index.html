About the Trust

Established in April 2026 as a charitable trust in Kozhikode, Kerala, Ashtapada Chess Academy aims to promote chess education and provide professional training to aspiring players of all ages.

Our Mission

To empower students through chess by enhancing concentration, analytical thinking, and decision-making skills.

Our Vision

To build a strong chess culture and create future champions while supporting academic and personal development.

Our Values

Excellence, discipline, inclusiveness, integrity, and social responsibility.
